# Governance
- Read-only discovery is the default; respect access controls, crawl limits and provider terms.
- Never send secrets, personal data or private customer records to research providers.
- Unknown metrics remain null. Every factual finding references evidence; estimates include provider and scope.
- Conflicting findings remain visible until reconciled; prefer first-party measurements for owned properties.
- Separate research approval, implementation approval and deployment approval. Approval for one is not approval for another.
- No link spam, doorway pages, fabricated reviews, cloaking or scaled low-value content.
- Record changes, rollback strategy and verification evidence before release.
- No guarantee of rankings or AI citations.
