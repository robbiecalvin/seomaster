# App Store Optimization

## Inputs
Project scope, upstream specialized findings, dated evidence and relevant URLs or records.

## Procedure
Compare store listing metadata, localization and conversion evidence; separate store from web rankings.
Perform evidence-based manual review; no automated helper is supplied.

## Deliverable
Store listing experiment plan
Include evidence IDs, affected scope, priority, owner and acceptance test. Mark missing inputs unavailable.

## Acceptance
Verify findings against source records, distinguish estimates from measurements, and obtain approval before changes. Never promise rankings or fabricate results.
